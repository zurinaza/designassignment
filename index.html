         Solid–liquid extraction
                      Extract vegetable oil with a solvent, not a press, and recover
                    every litre of that solvent.02

  Group 4                                      Group 5
  Soybean oil from flakes                Canola oil from pre-pressed cake
   Oil in feed 18–21 wt%                                    Oil in feed 15–20 wt%


Composition basis      Mass

Product                Residual oil in the meal  0.5 wt%

Target                     Oil recovery 97%

Throughput            5,000–20,000 kg/h of flakes or cake

Feed temperature      25–35 °C

Key constraint          Mechanical pressing excluded; the solvent must be recovered

Utilities              Steam at 400 kPa abs; cooling water at 30 °C

You choose              Solvent, extractor type, number of stages, solvent ratio


  Design notes
     Hexane is highly flammable (flash point about 22 °C): make it a node in your HAZOP.
      Solution retained in the underflow limits recovery; cite a measured value for your seed.
      In Aspen, model the hexane recovery section (miscella stripper, condenser, solvent
      recycle) and compare it with your stage balance.

     product.
